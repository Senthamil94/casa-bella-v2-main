About Sunol

Founded in 1871, Sunol is rich with history and scenery — a valley town between Pleasanton and Fremont.

The famous Sunol Water Temple rose in 1910 to celebrate three converging water sources; restored in 2001, it's now a beloved landmark and one of many storybook photo backdrops minutes from our door.
